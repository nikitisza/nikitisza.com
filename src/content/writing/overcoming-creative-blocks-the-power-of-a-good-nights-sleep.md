As a writer and designer, <strong>I’ve often hit roadblocks on my creative journey.</strong>

You know those times when you can’t get the words and designs right?  
I’ve been there <strong>*way too many times*</strong>.

<strong>My solution?</strong>

I learned to just sleep on it.

Surprisingly, after a good night’s rest, I often woke up with the solution magically clear in my mind & ready to tackle my creative projects.

Here’s something even more fascinating.

Have you ever had to recreate a lost piece of work from scratch?

I’ve been through this, too, and <strong>*something remarkable happens*</strong>.

The new version of my design and writing turned out better than the original.

This is because **our subconscious mind doesn’t stop working**. It refines and improves our ideas, even when we’re not actively thinking about them. \[[Source](https://www.dreams.co.uk/sleep-matters-club/7-ways-that-sleep-help-your-creativity#:~:text=The%20phenomenon%20attributed%20to%20sleep,turn%20aids%20creative%20problem%2Dsolving.)\]

<em>Next time you’re stuck in a creative slump? Sleep on it!</em>
