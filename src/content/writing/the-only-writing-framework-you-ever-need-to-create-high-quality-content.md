Start with empathy. Continue with utility. Improve with analysis. Optimize with love. — [Jonathon Colman](https://www.jonathoncolman.org/about/)

# Start with empathy

Your first ingredient is <em>understanding your audience’s needs, wants, and pain points.</em> This is your foundation, your <strong><em>‘flour’</em></strong> if you will. Without it, your content lacks substance and relevance.

# Continue with utility

Next, <em>add a generous helping of utility.</em> Your content should solve a problem or answer a question. This is the <strong><em>‘yeast’</em></strong> that makes your content rise above the rest.

# Improve with analysis

Once your foundation is done, <em>sprinkle in some analysis & metrics.</em>

-   Is it engaging?
-   Is it driving action?

This is your <strong><em>‘taste test.’</em></strong>

# Optimize with love

Finally, garnish your content with love. <em>Revisit, refine, and polish until it shines.</em> Think of this as the <strong><em>‘icing on the cake,’</em></strong> making good content great.

A simple yet effective framework to cook up <strong>content that not only satisfies but delights.</strong>
