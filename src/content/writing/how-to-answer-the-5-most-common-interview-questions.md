### What are the 5 most common interview questions

These questions are super important to answer well, especially when you’re starting out in the design industry as a junior designer. I found that I got these questions less often as I matured into my design career.

-   Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
-   Why do you want to work here?
-   Why did you leave your last role?
-   What is your greatest strength?
-   What is your greatest weakness?
