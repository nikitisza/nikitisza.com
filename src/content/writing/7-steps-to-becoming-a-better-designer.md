I’ve been asked many times how to become better at design.

Well, I do have a bulletproof formula that I’m going to share in this post.

Are you ready?

### **The secret**

… is a 7-step process

1\. Design.

2\. Design more.

3\. Design even more.

4\. Design when you want to.

5\. Design when you don’t want to.

6\. Design every day.

7\. Keep designing.

There you have it! 😁

Let’s remember...

> Nothing can replace hard work.

> Persistence, discipline & grit always beat talent. Always.
