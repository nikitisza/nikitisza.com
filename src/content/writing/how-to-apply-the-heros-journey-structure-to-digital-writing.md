In the study of storytelling, [**the hero’s journey**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero%27s_journey) is a blueprint for tales where the leading character embarks on a quest, conquers a challenge, and returns home as a changed or evolved individual.

Here’s how I simplify & apply [Campbell’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Campbell) <strong><em>hero’s journey for my digital writing.</em></strong>

# <strong>*The 8-step Structure*</strong>

## 1\. The Writer Encounters an Unexpected Obstacle

Ah, <strong>*life threw me a curveball*</strong>, and now I have a problem that needs solving.

## 2\. Building Strong Connections with the Reader

<strong>*I lay down some relatable groundwork*</strong> here so you and I are on the same page. I make the reader feel we're teammates in this adventure.

## 3\. Navigating Through Challenges

I’m <strong>*knee-deep in the problem-solving mud*</strong> but determined to get through it.

## 4\. Epiphanies That Illuminate the Path

<strong>*Lightbulb moments*</strong> are going off left and right. I've found a solution (or some solutions) that could be game-changers.

## 5\. Fearlessly Overcoming Adversity

With my newfound insights, <strong>*I’m tackling the issue like a pro*</strong> — imagine slaying a dragon by tapping away on your keyboard.

## 6\. Celebrating Success

This is victory time. <strong><em>I did it!</em></strong> The problem’s solved, and I’m doing my happy dance.

## 7\. Transferring Wisdom to the Next Generation

Now that I’ve navigated this maze, it’s time to share, so I hand you the treasure map. Now, <strong>*it’s your turn to conquer*</strong> and here’s how you can do it, too!

## 8\. Reflecting on the Journey

Before finishing (the book, the chapter, or the article), <strong>*I take a moment to look back at the wild ride*</strong>, to soak it all in, appreciating the highs and lows. I describe how my life has changed because of the journey.

# Conclusion

Following this structure, <strong>you’re taking your readers on a mini hero’s journey tailored to the digital age.</strong>

You’re not just solving a problem. You’re crafting an engaging, relatable narrative that offers value and wisdom.

And that is how you write a killer piece of digital content.
