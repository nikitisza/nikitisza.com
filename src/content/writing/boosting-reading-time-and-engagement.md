Believe it or not, a mere 30 seconds might not sound like an eternity, but in the fast-paced world of Medium, many readers don’t even dedicate that much time to our articles.

<strong>How can you make sure your readers stay on your article once clicked?</strong>

# 1 — Answer Burning Questions

Readers click on articles in search of answers.

They might start with a Google search and dive into your article that promises a solution to their problem.

To attract readers, make sure your article addresses common questions in your niche.

<strong><em>Where can you find burning questions?</em></strong>

1.  [Quora](https://www.quora.com/)
2.  [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/)
3.  [Answer the public](https://answerthepublic.com/)

Don’t forget to craft titles that mirror these questions for better visibility and clarity.

# 2 — Solve Problems

Similar to burning questions, your readers seek solutions to their problems.

Write content that tackles the issues in your area, in your niche. It’s always a good idea to back your solutions with credible sources to build trust. That credible source could be

-   You — tell your story, how you overcame a similar issue
-   A borrowed credibility — tell someone else’s story, how that person solved the same problem

# 3 — Leverage Your Expertise

<strong><em>People are drawn to writers with experience.</em></strong>

When you start your online writing journey, no one knows you, and it’ll take time to establish yourself as an expert in your field.

What I like to do is <strong><em>share what I’m learning.</em></strong> I don’t claim that I’m an expert in online writing, but interesting things happen when I share everything I know and learn while building my writing habit.

<strong>Guess what?</strong>

<em>It resonates with people!</em>

<strong><em>People love stories.</em></strong> Just genuinely sharing your experiences will boost your reading time and engagement.

# 4 — Offer Value

Readers want value they can apply immediately right after finishing your article.

**There are multiple ways to offer value**

-   Starting with a concise summary so readers know what to expect
-   Including takeaway paragraphs after each section in your blog post
-   Concluding with a final paragraph that ties everything together

<strong><em>Conclusion:</em></strong> <em>Your ability to answer questions, provide solutions, leverage your expertise, and consistently deliver value will keep your readers engaged in the whirlwind of online writing, so give it a go!</em>
