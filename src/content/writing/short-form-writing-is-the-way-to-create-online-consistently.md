Life’s rollercoaster & seasonal, right?

If you’re juggling a full-time job, a part-time writing gig, social life, pets & kids, life often feels like **a lot** and finding time to write can be tricky.

My new puppy’s training’s taken over my usual writing hours — 2 hours in the morning and 1 later. <em>Writing time’s on pause for now.</em>

But if you still crave a creative outlet, <strong>try short form content like me!</strong>

**It’ll help you**

-   Keep your writing habit alive
-   Show up in your readers’ inbox
-   Feel productive
-   Earn some extra cash

<strong>If you’re passionate about your writing gig & cannot not write,</strong> short form is a game changer.

You only need 30 minutes to whip up something of value. Trust me, it’s harder than it sounds to keep a story under 150 or 300 words.
