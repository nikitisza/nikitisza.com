Never discourage anyone who continually makes progress, no matter how slow… even if that someone is yourself! — Plato

September 2023 was an even better month financially on Medium than August! <strong>Who knew?</strong>

# I Had to Slow Down

September was the **best of the best** so far in my Medium journey, even though I had to slow down a bit.

I don’t want this Medium report to be a journal entry, but…

<strong>I got a new puppy!</strong>

<figure><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="/images/writing/september-was-about-growth-even-though-i-had-to-slow-down-medium-report-september-2023/651c7e2a3a534cd5f326f035_0-kswt2kbzuofielpt.jpeg"></div><figcaption>Lego, my toy poodle puppy — Image by the author</figcaption></figure>

Lego’s arrival is super exciting but also tiring. The 2 hours I spent every morning on writing are, <strong>poof, gone!</strong> Just like that.

I get up earlier, but the first 2 hours are not about writing anymore. Nowadays, I wake up, feed Lego, make a coffee, and play with her for 90 minutes. I know it sounds a lot, but she’s a *poodle*. Though I knew she’d be energetic, I wasn’t prepared for <strong><em>this level of energy.</em></strong>

Lots of sleepless and interrupted nights behind us. I just wish I could’ve taken these past two weeks off of work.

It became increasingly difficult to make time for writing. If anything, she forces me <strong><em>to be faster with writing & editing.</em></strong> I grab my laptop when I see a gap (because she’s asleep).

You’re not here for puppy talk, so let’s get to the juicy parts.

# Followers in September 2023

I closed the month with <strong><em>938 followers.</em></strong>

262 lovely people decided to follow my words. I just realized now that this account <strong>*will reach 1K in October*</strong>. It’s wild to think about it.

# Views in September 2023

September was even better than August in terms of views, too.

If you write at least 10 articles a month, you can get 6–8K views on your stories. And that’s <strong>*precisely*</strong> what happened to my stories.

If you read my [Medium report from August](https://medium.com/all-about-m/my-best-month-ever-on-medium-earning-over-100-medium-report-august-2023-ce835cb7ee1e), you know I did a commenting experiment that resulted in high views.

##### [In 7 Days, I Left 100 Comments on Medium Stories, and This Is What Happened to My Views](https://medium.com/all-about-m/in-7-days-i-left-100-comments-on-medium-stories-and-this-is-what-happened-to-my-views-1c5cbc9eaa86)

This month, I couldn’t do any experimentation, though.

I was just happy that I could publish <strong>*13 articles*</strong> (fewer than in the previous month) — I disappointed myself, but I need to accept the change that comes with the puppy. I’m happy that she’s here, but regardless, I thought I could just keep up my writing time. <strong>But no!</strong>

# Earnings in September 2023

Regardless of the smaller number of stories, my earnings went even higher than previously. <strong>Yay!</strong>

My MPP earnings have increased by more than $25 compared to August. <em>That’s an extra chew toy for Lego!</em>

# Articles Published in September 2023

I keep using my spreadsheet, which works well.

I added *2 extra columns* since last month. Now I also track how long the article is (word count) and how much time I spent on it overall, from idea to publishing.

<figure><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="/images/writing/september-was-about-growth-even-though-i-had-to-slow-down-medium-report-september-2023/651c7e2a0dc1528cfae38ba6_0-ha29j8lv9oegmjyp.png"></div><figcaption>My trusted spreadsheet — Image by the author</figcaption></figure>

As I mentioned, I published 13 articles in September. Most (9) of them are **shorter stories** with a maximum of 4 minutes of reading time. I had to embrace more short stories. It was the best I could do.

It’s actually more challenging writing something of value in 300 words or less. If you haven’t tried it yet, <strong><em>give it a go!</em></strong>

##### [Short Form Writing Is the Way To Create Online Consistently](https://medium.com/part-time-writer-club/short-form-writing-is-the-way-to-create-online-consistently-6f4a4e1928e4)

<strong>My idea bucket is bottomless!</strong>

I have many drafts in the making. It’s just the time I’m missing so much right now.

My favourite kind of articles, case studies, are coming together nicely. <strong>I have 3 in the making,</strong> and I can’t wait to share them with you all because they’ll be so helpful to those who do content creation or other side hustles.

# Publications

I only applied to 3 publications; they all accepted me as a writer, but I couldn’t publish anything in the pubs yet.

<strong>An unusual thing happened…</strong>

One of the publication’s editors deleted my submitted article. I only realized it a half day later. I messaged the pub owner, who apologized. I believe it was a genuine mistake from the editors’ side, but still, it was a sad event.

<strong>What’s the key message here?</strong>

<strong><em>Always have a backup of your stories!</em></strong> Don’t just have them saved in the Medium editor because hiccups can happen.

# What Else Did I Do To Drive Traffic to My Content?

I keep posting my stories on LinkedIn and X with little success. It only takes a few seconds, so I will keep doing it.

I’ve started posting some of my articles in Discord groups that I belong to. <strong>*I also feel guilty*</strong> because I couldn’t make enough time for it despite my desire to engage with the community.

My tech job is busy, too. I usually engage with the communities during my lunch break, but since Lego arrived, that 30 minutes goes to my puppy. It shows, too. People don’t engage with me and my articles that often.

I plan to return to the game as soon as I can find the time. <strong>I believe in reciprocity,</strong> so I don’t expect them to come clap and comment when I don’t do the same.

# Monthly Retro

## What’s working?

-   Writing short-form articles
-   Finding shorter 20 min creative time slots when I can write
-   Being laser-focused when I have time
-   Forcing myself to edit faster
-   Replying to comments

## What’s not working?

-   Writing and editing in the morning
-   Waiting for publications to publish my stories (some of them just take ages!) → I kind of want to do my own thing, but I don’t think my account is large enough to do it yet
-   Checking each notification
-   Spending too much time on engagement

## Engagement

Engaging with other people’s articles works, but I spent more time on engagement than writing, which has to change. Engagement is important, but these two shouldn’t be this off-balance.

**I spent 20.5 hours writing and editing Medium articles**, equalling <strong><em>9,220 words.</em></strong> Not bad, but it could’ve been better than this. Maybe in November, I can beat the number.

**I spent 25.5 hours on engagement**, including replying to comments, reading other writers’ articles, commenting on them, checking out other writers’ profiles, and engaging in Discord communities.

1.  Back up your articles!
2.  Have sharp-focused creative sprints for a short time.
3.  <strong><em>It’s OK if your schedule changes temporarily; just do your best.</em></strong>
