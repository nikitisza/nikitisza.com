Since I learned <strong>*The 4A Framework*</strong> in [Ship 30 for 30](https://www.ship30for30.com/) I never run out of ideas.

<strong>The framework’s four content types:</strong>

1.  <strong><em>Actionable:</em></strong> “Here’s how”
2.  <strong><em>Analytical:</em></strong> “Here’s what”
3.  <strong><em>Aspirational:</em></strong> “Yes, you can”
4.  <strong><em>Anthropological:</em></strong> “Here’s why”

Ship 30 teaches you to write <strong>*atomic content*</strong> and share it for feedback on X.

<strong>What does this mean?</strong>

**It means** sharing 1 piece of advice with your 3-years-ago self.

**It means** not sharing 5 or 7 pieces of advice with your 3-years-ago self.

Even if you have 7 pieces of advice, break them down and share them separately in their own atomic post, ensuring a steady flow of ideas.

<strong><em>Another example:</em></strong> instead of listing several books that influenced you on a certain topic, create individual posts for each book. This strategy not only provides clarity but also multiplies your content, turning what could be a single article into several impactful pieces.
