1.  Going to bed angry is okay! <strong><em>But don’t act when you’re angry.</em></strong> Seriously, sleep on it. You’ll see things in a new light in the morning.
2.  <strong><em>If you don’t ask, the answer will always be no.</em></strong> Be bold and ask! Ask for the promotion, ask for a salary raise; heck, even demand extra napkins and a free refill.
3.  <strong>*Don’t wait until you feel ready*</strong> because the chances are, you’ll probably never feel 100% ready. Just do things afraid.
4.  When someone is rude to you, it has more to do with them and their inner world than with you. <strong><em>It’s a sneak peek into their messy inner world.</em></strong> Don’t waste your brain cells on them.
5.  <strong>*Your attitude is the most important thing*</strong>; it’s your secret weapon.
6.  <strong><em>Smile!</em></strong> Always smile, even if you don’t want to. One smile can change other people’s attitudes toward you.
7.  <strong><em>Always be kind.</em></strong> It’s okay to be blunt and direct but deliver your feedback respectfully and kindly.
8.  <strong><em>Nobody has a clue what they’re doing!</em></strong> If it seems someone has it figured out, I promise you, they don’t. Most people just put on a facade and pretend.
9.  <strong><em>It’s okay not to want children on your own.</em></strong> If the thought of parenthood makes you cringe, don’t force it. There are plenty of other ways to be a positive influence and nurture younger generations.
10.  You don’t need to have the last words. You need to <strong><em>know when to shut up and say nothing.</em></strong> Some things are better left unsaid.
11.  Perfection is overrated. <strong>*Aim for progress*</strong> and just show up every day.
12.  You don’t need to forgive, but you need to <strong>*learn how to let things go*</strong> because if you don’t, it’ll hurt you every time you think about the person or the event.
13.  Money is cool, but all the <strong>*valuable things can’t be paid for*</strong>, e.g. kindness, solid friendships, patience, trust, and love.
14.  <strong><em>Focus is more important than anything else to reach a goal.</em></strong> It’s okay to have multiple passions and lots of different activities you love in your life. But if you want to build something around your passion (e.g., a business), you need to focus on one thing for a longer period to know you gave it everything you could. Then, feel free to move on to something else when you itch for a change.
15.  There’s enough room for everyone. <strong><em>Everyone can have a slice of the ‘success cake.’</em></strong> Just because someone does well in their chosen profession or life doesn’t mean you lose anything.
16.  <strong><em>Don’t see other women as competition.</em></strong> Make friends with them. There’s too much girl-on-girl hate out there. Let’s be the change and lift each other up.
17.  Cultivate your <strong>*inner peace*</strong>, whatever it takes. Do whatever it takes to find yours.
18.  <strong>*Don’t follow dogmas and people*</strong> who say there’s only one way to do a certain thing. They are most likely wrong.
19.  <strong>*Most things in life don’t matter in 5 or 10 years*</strong>, so pick your battles.
20.  It’s okay to <strong>*set boundaries*</strong>, even with your family.
21.  <strong><em>Just because you’re sad about a particular decision doesn’t mean it was wrong.</em></strong> Give it some time.
22.  <strong><em>Choosing your life partner is one of your most important decisions.</em></strong> Pick a good one who’s kind, supportive, trustworthy, and shares the same values. Attraction is good if you have it, but don’t pick a person based on only that. <strong><em>Because when the attraction is gone, what’s left?</em></strong>
23.  <strong><em>Happiness isn’t a constant state.</em></strong> It comes and goes. Try to find happy moments in your days. I promise there’s always one thing, so focus on that.
24.  <strong><em>Always be over-prepared.</em></strong> Shit happens, e.g. how would you deliver your presentation if tech fails?
25.  You can trust only yourself.
26.  The best habit you can develop is <strong>*discipline*</strong>.
27.  Health is everything. Take care of your health by eating mostly plants, exercising daily, sleeping at least 7 hours, drinking 2 litres of water, and limiting alcohol intake. Diets don’t work. <strong><em>A good night’s sleep and water can cure many things.</em></strong>
28.  <strong><em>Never stop learning.</em></strong>
29.  Don’t worry about what others think of you. They are beyond your control. <strong><em>Do you!</em></strong>
30.  <strong><em>You’re not your job.</em></strong> A job title doesn’t define anyone. Don’t pay too much attention to people with fancy titles. They are no better than a janitor in your building.
31.  <strong><em>Life is better with pets.</em></strong> I always like having a dog around.
32.  Spend time in nature. Take long walks. It’s good for you.
33.  <strong><em>Trust yourself and your intuition.</em></strong> If something feels off, likely, things are off; your gut usually knows.
34.  You can get far ahead by being a good conversationalist and good at communicating.
35.  Remember to <strong>*wear sunscreen*</strong>!
36.  A combination of journaling and meditation is the best way to understand yourself, your feelings, and your thoughts.
37.  <strong><em>Don’t overwork yourself!</em></strong> Whenever you feel burnout approaching, slow down to recharge.
38.  If you no longer feel passionate about a topic or person, it’s okay to change your mind and move on.
39.  Life doesn’t come with a pre-defined purpose. We’re all just living creatures on this planet, striving to make the most of our existence. But when you zoom out, <strong><em>nothing we do holds any significance, and nothing matters in the grand scheme.</em></strong> This thought can be depressing, but once you get over that feeling, it’s pretty freeing because it makes you fuss less about stuff.
40.  <strong>*Nothing is inherently good or bad*</strong>; they simply just are. It’s all a matter of perspective.
