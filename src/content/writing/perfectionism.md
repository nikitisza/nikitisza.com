As designers we are often labeled as perfectionists.

Thriving for perfectionism can derail productivity & cause anxiety.

Once we understand perfectionism is not a desired trait, and we can be good enough without being perfect….then, we can have a nice, happy time in life.

Would you like to let go of perfectionism? Watch the video until the end.  

This video aims to calm...to give a little bit of 'zen' in this busy life.
