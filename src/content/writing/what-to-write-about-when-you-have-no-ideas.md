# Write about

-   Your travel
-   Trending topics
-   Your pet peeves
-   What frustrates you
-   Your passion or hobby
-   What changed your life
-   What makes you angry
-   What makes you happy
-   Challenges and how you overcame
-   A discovery you made during your life
-   Someone else’s life for borrowed credibility
-   A controversial topic with your point of view
-   Your recent or past experience that made you think
-   Other people’s life story (parents, siblings, friends, colleagues)

For me, my unconventional childhood is a constant source of ideas, and <strong><em>I bet yours too.</em></strong>

To have ideas, you need to live.

It’s as simple as that.

<strong>*Ideas aren’t born in a vacuum*</strong> because they’re the offspring of experiences, interactions, and frustrations. Go live!
