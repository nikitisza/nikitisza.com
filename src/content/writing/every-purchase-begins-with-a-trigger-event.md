# Trigger Events That Stimulate Buyers’ Purchasing Decision

If you’re selling a service or product online, you can anticipate and tap into these trigger events to enhance your sales, so that’s why it’s important to understand the different triggers.

## 1 — Personal Triggers

<strong>Life stage changes:</strong> A person gets married and decides to buy a home. Or a new baby arrives, and it’s time for a larger car.

<strong>Achievements:</strong> Graduating from college can lead to purchasing professional attire for job interviews.

## 2 — External Triggers

<strong>Seasonal changes:</strong> The onset of winter prompts someone to buy a new coat or heating system.

<strong>Economic fluctuations:</strong> A booming economy might see increased luxury goods purchases, while downturns might spur interest in cost-saving solutions.

## 3 — Product-Related Triggers

<strong>End of life cycle:</strong> A laptop slows down after several years, prompting the need for a new one.

<strong>New product launches:</strong> The release of a new iPhone model triggers loyal Apple users to upgrade.

## 4 — Situational Triggers

<strong>Relocation:</strong> Moving to a new country necessitates buying new furniture or appliances suited for the new country.

<strong>Change in job or role:</strong> Getting a new job requiring frequent travel can lead to purchasing quality luggage.

## 5 — Emotional Triggers

<strong>Desire for status:</strong> Wanting the latest luxury handbag or sports car to flaunt one’s status.

<strong>Solving pain points:</strong> Having persistent back pain leads someone to buy an ergonomic chair.

## 6 — Social Triggers

<strong>Peer recommendations:</strong> A friend raves about their new smart home system, making you consider getting one, too.

<strong>Social media trends:</strong> A product going viral on platforms like TikTok or Instagram can create a sudden surge in demand.

## 7 — Problem or Incident Triggers

<strong>Break or malfunction:</strong> Your fridge breaks down suddenly, making you buy a new one.

<strong>Safety concerns:</strong> News about increasing break-ins in a neighbourhood can lead residents to purchase security systems.

## 8 — Educational Triggers

<strong>Awareness through content:</strong> Reading a detailed article about the benefits of an air purifier can convince someone of its necessity.

<strong>Workshops or webinars:</strong> Attending a seminar about vegan eating can lead to a spike in more plant-based food purchases.

## 9 — Promotional Triggers

<strong>Sales or discounts:</strong> Black Friday deals can prompt consumers to buy products they’ve been eyeing for months.

<strong>Loyalty programs:</strong> Earning enough loyalty points might encourage the next purchase to redeem those points.
