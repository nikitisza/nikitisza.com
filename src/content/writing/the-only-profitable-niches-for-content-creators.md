If you want to make money online, there are only <strong>*4 topics*</strong> that make your content profitable:

-   Health
-   Wealth
-   Relationships
-   Happiness

<strong>Why?</strong>

Because these are the only things <strong>*everyone cares about*</strong>. That’s why they universally resonate with an audience.

Take note of <strong>*successful content creators*</strong> like

-   [*Dan Koe*](https://thedankoe.com/)
-   [*Zulie Rane*](https://medium.com/@zulie)
-   [*Tim Denning*](https://medium.com/@timdenning)
-   [*Ayo Awosika*](https://medium.com/@chef-boyardeji)
-   [*Sinem Günel*](https://sinemgnel.medium.com/)

These exceptional writers excel by integrating their personal stories and unique perspectives into these niches. Their approach to <strong><em>‘nicheless entrepreneurship’</em></strong> essentially forms a distinct niche around their individual experiences and viewpoints so that they become <strong><em>‘the niche.’</em></strong>

Each article they write offers insights into their <strong><em>origin story, beginnings, challenges, strategies, and shows their world through their lens.</em></strong> This personal angle makes their content uniquely engaging and relatable.

<strong>Their success, regardless of the topic, is a mix of authenticity & relatability, rooted in personal experience,</strong> and this is how they monetize their content online.
