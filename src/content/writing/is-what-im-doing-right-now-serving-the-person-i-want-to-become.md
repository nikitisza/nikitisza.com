<em>Are you on a journey of self-improvement?</em>

<em>Do you want to be more focused?</em>

If you’ve a side hustle or want to improve daily, you must practise **self-reflection**.

As [Neil Strauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Strauss) says

<strong><em>Question to ask yourself many times a day:</em></strong>

<strong><em>Is what I’m doing right now serving the person I want to become?</em></strong>

This quote has been a beacon, especially since turning 40.

Lately, I’ve noticed an increased tendency to get distracted, a phenomenon that I attribute to aging and the aftereffect of Covid.

Recognizing this, I understood the importance of frequent <strong>*self-assessment*</strong>.

<strong>It’s not enough to set intentions once a day.</strong>

We must revisit them repeatedly, making this introspection a part of our routine.

<strong>Multiple times a day.</strong>

To help this, I created a vision board & set it as my laptop wallpaper, ensuring my goals are always in plain sight.

<strong><em>What’s your plan for 2024?</em></strong>
