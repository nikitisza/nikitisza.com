Ever feel overwhelmed by the whirlwind of digital content creation?

<strong>You’re not alone.</strong>

There was a time when I consumed <strong>*way too much*</strong> of [Gary’s](https://garyvaynerchuk.com/) content. But one of his gems stayed with me forever.

# His advice?

Dive deep and <strong>create 100 pieces of content before you even think of hitting the brakes.</strong>

Sounds intense?

Maybe, but <strong>*there’s a logic*</strong> to his madness.

Most of us get disheartened if our first few articles or videos don’t skyrocket.

<strong><em>Gary’s point is clear:</em></strong> once you’ve hustled through those 100, you’ll truly grasp what your audience wants because you’ll have enough data. Each article, each video is a lesson, a piece of the puzzle.

His mantra <strong>isn’t just about consistency.</strong>

<strong>It’s about resilience.</strong>

By that 100th content, you’ll not only understand your audience better but also yourself. Only after that can you assess whether it’s worth pushing forward.

<strong>Never stop creating!</strong>
