### YouTube related things

In case you're interested, here are some videos I made in October:

I’ve started a new video series about my web design process - to be precise, website redesign process, so I thought I’d document [my website](https://www.nikitisza.com/) redesign process. Here’s the first episode, in which I discuss Google Analytics, Canva, and Webflow insights: [link to YouTube video](https://youtu.be/GMuKNvR_Jlw).

There are numerous analytics and tracking tools available to help you better understand user behaviour. I simply prefer to use GA. If you're unfamiliar with it or how to get started, but would like to learn more about your users/customers, Google offers free training: [link to Google Analytics training.](https://analytics.google.com/analytics/academy/)

As a product/UX/UI designer, you may be asked to conduct quantitative user research, and I believe GA is ideal for this type of data collection. When I can, I always prefer data-drive design.

One of my most controversial videos to date...why junior designers have a difficult time finding work. I'd love to hear your thoughts...[link to video](https://youtu.be/MFW2iQtlsdQ).

My favourite video of the month has to be this: [UX design explained in 2 min](https://youtu.be/0HP0kGrAszI).
