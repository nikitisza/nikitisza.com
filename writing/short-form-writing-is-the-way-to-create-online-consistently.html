Short Form Writing Is the Way To Create Online Consistently

Easy way to show up

Life’s rollercoaster & seasonal, right?

If you’re juggling a full-time job, a part-time writing gig, social life, pets & kids, life often feels like a lot and finding time to write can be tricky.

My new puppy’s training’s taken over my usual writing hours — 2 hours in the morning and 1 later. Writing time’s on pause for now.

But if you still crave a creative outlet, try short form content like me!

It’ll help you

  • Keep your writing habit alive
  • Show up in your readers’ inbox
  • Feel productive
  • Earn some extra cash

If you’re passionate about your writing gig & cannot not write, short form is a game changer.

You only need 30 minutes to whip up something of value. Trust me, it’s harder than it sounds to keep a story under 150 or 300 words.

Where to publish?

  • Shortform
  • Midform
  • The Daily Cuppa

Give short form writing a shot!
