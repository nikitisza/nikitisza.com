I’ve started a new video series about my web design process - to be precise, website redesign process, so I thought I’d document my website redesign process. Here’s the first episode, in which I discuss Google Analytics, Canva, and Webflow insights: link to YouTube video.

There are numerous analytics and tracking tools available to help you better understand user behaviour. I simply prefer to use GA. If you're unfamiliar with it or how to get started, but would like to learn more about your users/customers, Google offers free training: link to Google Analytics training.

As a product/UX/UI designer, you may be asked to conduct quantitative user research, and I believe GA is ideal for this type of data collection. When I can, I always prefer data-drive design.
